Privacy Policy: Eksoch Invoice Maker

Last updated 24 November 2026

Your data stays on your device. Your invoices, quotations, clients, business details, logo and signature are stored only in this app's storage on your device. We don't run a server that receives them, and we can't see them.

What leaves your device

  • Pro license checks. If you enter a Pro license key, the app sends that key and the product ID to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm your subscription is active, about once a day while you're online. Gumroad returns whether the subscription is active and the email used to buy it. See Gumroad's privacy policy.
  • AppSumo codes. If you enter an AppSumo code, it is checked inside the app on your device and saved only there. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Email. The Email button opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
  • Backups and PDFs are files saved to your device, wherever you choose.

No tracking

The app has no ads, analytics or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all data, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

Contact

Questions: reach us through our Gumroad store, eksochanalytics.gumroad.com.
